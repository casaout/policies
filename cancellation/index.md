# Cancellation policy

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

We want satisfied customers, not hostages. That's why we make it easy for you to cancel directly in the app — no phone calls required, no questions asked.

There are two things a team owner can do, depending on what you want:

* **Cancel your subscription** to stop being charged. Go to your team's billing settings and cancel your subscription. Your content is kept.
* **Delete your team** to remove its content. Under your team settings, scroll to the bottom of the page and press the "Delete" button. A modal will pop up asking you to confirm.

Our legal responsibility is to team owners, which means we cannot cancel or delete a team at the request of anyone else. If for whatever reason you no longer know who the team owner is, [contact us](https://engineawesome.com/support/). We will gladly reach out to any current team owners at the email addresses we have on file.

## What happens when you cancel your subscription?

We won't bill you again once you cancel. When your subscription ends, your team is locked: nobody can use it, but we keep its content, so you can subscribe again and pick up where you left off. If you want the content removed too, delete the team.

If you cancel during a free trial, you won't be charged when the trial ends.

We don't automatically prorate any unused time you may have left but if you haven't used your account in months or just started a new billing cycle, [contact us](https://engineawesome.com/support/) for a [fair refund](/about/policies/refunds/). We'll treat you right.

## What happens when you delete a team?

You won't be able to access your team once you delete it, so make sure you download everything you want to keep beforehand.

We'll permanently delete the content in your team from our servers 30 days after you delete it, and from our backups within 60 days. Unless you subscribed to and used our backup service, retrieving content for a single team from a backup isn't possible, so if you change your mind you'll need to contact us within the first 30 days. Backups made with our backup service are saved to your own storage, so deleting a team doesn't delete them. **Content can't be recovered once it has been permanently deleted.**

## Engine Awesome-initiated cancellations

We may cancel accounts if they have been inactive for an extended period:
* For locked accounts: 180 days after being locked because a payment failed
* For free accounts: after 365 days of inactivity

We also retain the right to suspend or terminate accounts for any reason at any time, as outlined in our [Terms of Service](/about/policies/terms-and-conditions/). In practice, this generally means we will cancel your account without notice if we have evidence that you are using our products to engage in [abusive behavior](/about/policies/use-restrictions/).
