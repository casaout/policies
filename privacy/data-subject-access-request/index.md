# Data Subject Access Request

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

## Overview

You have rights over your personal data. This page explains how to exercise those rights under various privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and others.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **Access** - Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- **Deletion** - Request that we delete your personal data
- **Correction** - Request that we correct inaccurate data
- **Portability** - Request your data in a machine-readable format
- **Opt-out** - Opt out of certain data processing activities
- **Non-discrimination** - Exercise your rights without discrimination

## How to Submit a Request

To exercise your data rights, contact us with the following information:

### Required Information

1. **Your full name** (first and last)
2. **Email address** used to access Engine Awesome
3. **Legal framework** for your request:
   - GDPR (European Union / EEA)
   - CCPA (California, USA)
   - Other applicable regulation
4. **Your request** - Specific details about what action you're requesting

### Request Types

You can request one of the following:

- Access to collected personal information
- Deletion of your data
- Opt out from third-party data sales
- Opt in to personal data sales
- Other requests (please describe)

## Important Notices

Before submitting a request, please understand:

1. **Accuracy** - You must certify that the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge
2. **Irreversibility** - Data deletion is permanent and may result in termination of services
3. **Verification** - We may need to verify your identity before processing your request

## Processing Time

- We aim to respond to all requests within **30 days**
- Complex requests may take up to **45 days** with notice
- We may contact you for additional information if needed
