# Company processors

*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

We as a company use third-party software that may process your information under certain circumstances.

* [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/gdpr/introduction/) Content delivery network and DDoS mitigation.
* [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy) Email newsletter service.
* [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/gdpr-readiness-requirements) Payment transfer service.
* [Piwik PRO](https://piwik.pro/privacy-policy/) Website analytics.

As a company, we host a blog, and run occasional events virtually. If you voluntarily engage with us through those media, your personal information may also be collected by the following processors:

* [X (formerly Twitter)](https://gdpr.x.com/) Social media platform.
* [Zoom](https://explore.zoom.us/en/trust/legal-compliance/) Video meetings and webinars.
